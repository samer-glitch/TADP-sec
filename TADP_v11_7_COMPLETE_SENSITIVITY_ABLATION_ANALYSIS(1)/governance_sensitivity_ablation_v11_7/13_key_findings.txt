Baseline thresholds: T_R=3.00, T_A=4.00, T_C_direct=4.00, T_C_review=3.50, C_min=3.00

Interpretation safeguards:
- Percent perturbations are robustness/stress tests, not newly optimized policies.
- C_min remains fixed at ADEQUATE=3 because it is an ordinal semantic non-compensatory safeguard.
- T_C_direct follows T_A.
- T_C_review remains (T_R + T_A)/2.
- Weight perturbations preserve sum(weights)=1 by proportional rescaling.
- LODO removes the omitted dimension from HPS and, when applicable, from the critical set.

Greatest observed LODO admission impact: Data Quality (6 admission flips; Δ admitted=-6).
Greatest observed LODO mean absolute HPS impact: Data Quality (mean |ΔHPS|=0.5050).
Greatest observed admission impact among dimensions designated critical: Data Quality (6 admission flips).

Do not interpret these rankings as universal causal importance.
They describe observed sensitivity in this controlled 20-client experimental cohort.
